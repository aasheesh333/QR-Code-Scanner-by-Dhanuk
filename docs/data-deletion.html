Data Deletion

QuickScan Pro: QR & Barcode — Dhanuk Softwares · Last updated: August 2026

What data the app stores

  • Scan history (scanned QR codes and barcodes), generated QR codes, QR templates, and vault items are stored only on your device in a local database. This data is never transmitted to Dhanuk Softwares or any server.
  • The app includes Google AdMob (advertising), Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics), and Firebase Analytics (usage statistics). These Google SDKs process anonymized device and app-usage data under Google's own retention and privacy policies — see Google Privacy Policy.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal identifiers.

How to delete your data

  1. Inside the app: open History and delete individual scans or batches, or use the clear/delete-all option to remove your entire scan history, generated QR codes, and vault items. This removes the data from your device permanently.
  2. Uninstall the app: uninstalling QuickScan Pro from Android Settings removes all data stored locally by the app.
  3. Request deletion by email: email support[provider]uksoftwares.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request — QuickScan Pro". We will process your request within 30 days.

Ads & analytics data

  • To stop personalized advertising and reset ad-related identifiers on your device, go to Android Settings → Google → Ads and opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Crash and usage statistics sent to Firebase are anonymized, are not linked to your identity, and are retained and deleted according to Google's data retention policies.
