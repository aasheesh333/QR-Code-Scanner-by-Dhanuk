Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QuickScan Pro ("we", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Camera and Photos: The App uses your device camera to scan QR codes and barcodes. Images are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server unless you explicitly choose to share them.

Scan History: The App stores your scan history locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Device Information: We collect anonymous device information (device model, OS version, app version) for analytics and crash reporting via Firebase Crashlytics.

Push Notification Token: We use OneSignal to send push notifications. A unique device token is stored to deliver notifications to your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide QR code scanning and generation features
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To send push notifications about app updates and features
  • To analyze app usage and improve user experience
  • To diagnose and fix crashes via Firebase Crashlytics

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob: Used to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Google Privacy Policy

Firebase Crashlytics: Used for crash reporting and analytics. Firebase Privacy

OneSignal: Used for push notifications. OneSignal Privacy Policy

4. Permissions Used

  • Camera: For scanning QR codes and barcodes
  • Storage: For saving generated QR codes and importing images
  • Internet: For ads, analytics, and push notifications
  • Vibrate: For haptic feedback during scanning
  • Notifications: For receiving push notifications

5. Data Storage and Security

All scan data is stored locally on your device. We do not store your personal data on external servers. Data transmitted to third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, OneSignal) is encrypted in transit.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You can clear all app data by uninstalling the App or clearing data from device settings. You can disable push notifications from your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@dhanuksoftwares.com
