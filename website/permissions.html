Permissions Used by QuickScan Pro

QuickScan Pro requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality. Below is a detailed explanation of each permission and how it is used.

1. Camera Permission

Permission: android.permission.CAMERA

Purpose: Used to scan QR codes and barcodes using your device camera.

Data Usage: Camera frames are processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit. Images are not uploaded or stored on any server.

2. Storage / Media Access

Permission: android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (API 32 and below)

Purpose: Used for importing images from your gallery to scan QR codes from images, and saving generated QR codes.

Data Usage: Only files explicitly selected by the user are accessed. We do not read or access files automatically.

3. Internet Permission

Permission: android.permission.INTERNET

Purpose: Used for displaying advertisements (Google AdMob), crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), and push notifications (OneSignal).

4. Vibrate Permission

Permission: android.permission.VIBRATE

Purpose: Provides haptic feedback when a QR code is successfully scanned.

5. Post Notifications

Permission: android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: Used to display push notifications from OneSignal about app updates and features.

Opt-out: You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

6. WiFi State

Permission: android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_STATE

Purpose: Used for WiFi QR code actions - joining WiFi networks from scanned WiFi QR codes.

Permissions We Do NOT Use

  • We do not access your contacts
  • We do not access your location
  • We do not access your microphone
  • We do not read your SMS or call logs
  • We do not upload your personal data to our servers

Data Stays On Your Device

All scan history, generated QR codes, and app settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data.
