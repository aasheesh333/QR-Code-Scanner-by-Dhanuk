Permissions Used by QuickScan Pro

QuickScan Pro requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality. Below is a detailed explanation of each permission and how it is used.

1. Camera Permission

Permission: android.permission.CAMERA

Purpose: Used to scan QR codes and barcodes using your device camera.

Data Usage: Camera frames are processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit. Images are not uploaded or stored on any server.

2. Storage / Media Access

Permission: android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (API 32 and below)

Purpose: Used for importing images from your gallery to scan QR codes from images, and saving generated QR codes.

Data Usage: Only files explicitly selected by the user are accessed. We do not read or access files automatically.

3. Internet Permission

Permission: android.permission.INTERNET

Purpose: Used for displaying advertisements (Google AdMob), crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), and push notifications (OneSignal).

4. Vibrate Permission

Permission: android.permission.VIBRATE

Purpose: Provides haptic feedback when a QR code is successfully scanned.

5. Post Notifications

Permission: android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: Used to display push notifications from OneSignal about app updates and features.

Opt-out: You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

6. WiFi State

Permission: android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_STATE

Purpose: Used for WiFi QR code actions - reading the current WiFi network name for WiFi QR generation, and joining WiFi networks from scanned WiFi QR codes.

7. Location (for WiFi SSID)

Permission: android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Purpose: Required by Android 10+ (API 29) to read your current WiFi network name (SSID) when generating WiFi QR codes.

Data Usage: We do NOT track your GPS location. This permission is only used to read the WiFi network you are currently connected to.

8. Calendar

Permission: android.permission.READ_CALENDAR, android.permission.WRITE_CALENDAR

Purpose: Used to save calendar events (VEVENT) directly from scanned QR codes into your default calendar app.

Data Usage: Calendar access is only triggered when you scan a calendar-type QR code and explicitly choose to save the event.

9. Biometrics

Permission: android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC

Purpose: Used to unlock the Secure Vault feature and protect sensitive scans with fingerprint or face authentication.

Permissions We Do NOT Use

  • We do not access your microphone
  • We do not read your SMS, phone state, or call logs
  • We do not upload your personal data to our servers

Advertising & Analytics

QuickScan Pro uses Google AdMob which may access the device advertising identifier for ad personalization (subject to your consent). Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as screen views and sessions to improve the app. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash traces. OneSignal collects push device tokens to deliver notifications.

All ad and analytics behavior is gated behind Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow for users in the European Economic Area.

Data Stays On Your Device

All scan history, generated QR codes, vaulted items, and app settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data. Cloud backups can be disabled in your device settings.
