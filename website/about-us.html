About the App

QuickScan Pro is a comprehensive QR code scanner and generator app for Android devices. It allows you to:

  • Scan QR codes and barcodes instantly using your device camera
  • Generate custom QR codes with various styles and colors
  • View and manage scan history with favorites
  • Batch scan multiple codes in sequence
  • Look up product information from barcodes
  • View analytics of your scanning activity

Key Features

  • Fast and accurate scanning powered by Google ML Kit
  • Beautiful Material Design 3 interface with dark/AMOLED themes
  • Works offline - no internet required for scanning
  • Privacy-first - all scan data stays on your device
  • Smart actions based on scanned content (open URLs, call numbers, etc.)
